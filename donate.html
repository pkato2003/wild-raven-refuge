Where the money goes

Every dollar, itemised.

These are our actual 2025 figures, rounded to the nearest dollar.

  • Veterinary care — 34% — $41,280 X-rays, surgery, bloodwork, medication, euthanasia
  • Food & formula — 26% — $31,566 Hand-rearing formula, insects, mice, fish, produce
  • Equipment & pens — 18% — $21,853 Wire, lumber, heat lamps, incubators, netting, repairs
  • Transport — 12% — $14,568 Fuel, vehicle maintenance, carriers, mileage
  • Permits & insurance — 7% — $8,497 Federal and state permits, liability insurance
  • Administration — 3% — $3,641 Website, PO box, accounting, bank fees

Total 2025 expenditure: $121,405

Other ways to help

Not everyone can give money.

Volunteer

We need drivers, feeders, laundry-doers, pen-builders and people willing to sit quietly in a dark room and do nothing. Minimum four hours a month.

Email the volunteer team

Donate supplies

Old towels, newspapers, cardboard boxes, unscented laundry detergent, paper towels, zip ties. All of it gets used. None of it is too small.

Employer matching

Many employers match charitable donations dollar for dollar. If yours does, a $50 gift becomes $100 at no extra cost to you. Ask your HR department.

Accountability

What we promise you.

  • We publish full financial statements every year, including salaries (there are none) and every expense over $500.
  • We never sell, share or trade your contact details. Ever.
  • We never use a bird’s photo to raise money for anything other than that bird’s care.
  • We will tell you honestly if a donation is better sent somewhere else.
  • If you ask where your money went, we will tell you, specifically.
