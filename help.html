Found a raven or crow?

The first hour matters.

Four rules. Tap each one to read.

Call (541) 214-0198

Cat bite? Call us immediately. Twelve hours is the difference between treatment and euthanasia.

01Box it. Dark, quiet, warm.→

Cardboard box with air holes in the lid. Folded towel on the bottom.

Somewhere dark, quiet and warm. No radio, no TV, no children, no pets.

02Do not feed it or give water.→

Food rots in the crop of a shocky bird. Water goes into the lungs. This is the most important rule.

03Do not splint or wrap anything.→

A wing set wrong fuses wrong. A leg wrapped too tight loses circulation in four hours.

04Call us. Any hour.→

(541) 214-0198, any hour. If we can’t take the bird, we will tell you who can.

Before you pick it up

Is it actually orphaned?

Leave it alone if…→
  • Fully feathered, short tail, stubby wings
  • Can hop and hold its head up
  • An adult crow or raven is nearby
  • Adults are bringing it food
Call us if…→
  • Naked, or only down, no real feathers
  • Lying flat, can’t right itself
  • No adult in two hours of watching
  • Visible blood, drooping wing, bad leg
  • Brought in by a cat or dog
