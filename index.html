Our residents

Frank, Jackcrow & Chali.

Raven

Frank

Fractured wing. 63 days. Released.

Read Frank’s story →

Found on Coburg Road, two days down. Clean fracture through the left humerus. Pinned the next morning. Four weeks recovery, five in the flight pen. Released 16 January.

Crow

Jackcrow

Nestling. 47 days. Released.

Read Jackcrow’s story →

Nine days old when he came in. No feathers, no eyes open. Fed every ninety minutes for three weeks. Fledged in April. Still visits the original garden.

Raven

Chali

Lead poisoning. 98 days. Released.

Read Chali’s story →

Found near Junction City, unable to stand. Lead at 74 µg/dL. Two chelation courses, eleven weeks of feeding. Released in March.
