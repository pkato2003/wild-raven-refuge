Raven · 63 days

Frank

Fractured humerus. Released.

Read the full story →

Found on Coburg Road, two days down. A clean fracture through the left humerus. Dr. Okonkwo pinned the wing the next morning. Four weeks recovery, five in the flight pen. He bit through one leather glove. Released 16 January, same location.

Crow · 47 days

Jackcrow

Nestling. Released.

Read the full story →

Nine days old when he came in. No feathers, no eyes open. Formula every ninety minutes for three weeks. Fledged in April and released into the same garden he came from. Still visits most mornings.

Raven · 98 days

Chali

Lead poisoning. Released.

Read the full story →

Found near Junction City, unable to stand. Blood lead 74 µg/dL. Two chelation courses, eleven weeks of syringe-feeding, slow re-flight training. Released in March, ninety-eight days after intake.
